Health
Every New Zealander should be able to get timely, high-quality healthcare when they need it. Treatment should be based on individual need, regardless of background, and the system should be judged by what it delivers for patients.
With ACT in Government, we have abolished the Māori Health Authority, removed race-based provisions from health law, required measurable health targets, and opened more opportunities for private providers to cut waiting lists. But there is much more to do. ACT is campaigning to close the medicines gap with Australia, let pharmacies provide more everyday care, and strengthen End of Life Choice.
ACT supports a competitive, innovative health sector in which public, private, and community providers work together. Our focus is on cutting waste, improving accountability, and making sure every taxpayer dollar goes where it will make the greatest difference for patients.
