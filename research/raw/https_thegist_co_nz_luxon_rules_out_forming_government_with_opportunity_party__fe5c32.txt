Luxon formally rules out coalition with Opportunity Party

National leader Christopher Luxon has formally ruled out forming a governing arrangement with the Opportunity Party after the election.

THE GIST
JULY 27, 2026

Prime Minister Christopher Luxon has formally ruled out any post-election deal with the Opportunity Party, citing their policies on taxes including a universal income and land tax. His office confirmed the statement amounted to a formal ruling out after he told media he wouldn't work with parties wanting to increase taxes. The Opportunity Party leader says her party remains open to conversations, while Labour has not yet declared its position on potential partners. Polling shows the Opportunity Party hovering near the 5% threshold, with recent surveys suggesting a small shift in their support could significantly alter the balance of power in a future Parliament.
