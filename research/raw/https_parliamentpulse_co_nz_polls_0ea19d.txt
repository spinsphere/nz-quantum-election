PUBLISHED RECORD

Conducted by RNZ/Reid Research · sample 1,000 · margin ±3.1%

RNZ–Reid Research poll, conducted online 2–9 July 2026 (n=1,000; maximum MoE ±3.1% at 95% confidence), published 14 July 2026. Quota sampled and weighted by age, gender and geography. Labour 34% (-1.6), National 28.7% (-2.1), NZ First 11.5% (+0.9), Green 10.3% (+0.2), ACT 7.8% (+0.8), Opportunity/TOP 4.7% (+2.7), Te Pāti Māori 2.3% (-0.9). RNZ projects the coalition at 61 seats and the left bloc at 59, assuming no overhang and the return of all current parliamentary parties. Opportunity remains 0.3 points below the 5% threshold.
