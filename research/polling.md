## 7. Seat-allocation rules and 2023 baseline
- 120 nominal seats; Sainte-Lague allocation of party-vote shares among parties passing the threshold (5% party vote or one electorate seat); electorate winners are deducted from the party's entitlement and any excess is overhang (House expands).
- Electorate count: source said 71 electorate + 49 list; I believe 72 electorates (65 general + 7 Maori) were used in 2023 - UNVERIFIED, discrepancy.
- 2023 result: National 48 (38.06%), Labour 34 (26.91%), Green 15 (11.60%), ACT 11 (8.64%), NZ First 8 (6.08%), TPM 6 (3.08%); total 123 (3 overhang from TPM). A Wikipedia election-page summary listed National as 49 "current" seats and TPM 4 "current" (mid-term changes, UNVERIFIED).
