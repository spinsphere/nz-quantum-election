# NZ Labour Party - 2026 Election Policy Research

Research date: 2026-10-06. Election: 7 November 2026.
Method note: WebSearch budget was exhausted at the start of this session, so research used WebFetch on labour.org.nz policy pages and RNZ pages only. No independent (Treasury/NZIER/Infometrics etc.) analysis of individual policies could be retrieved; where absent this is stated. Items not confirmed on a primary Labour page or RNZ are marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Health and Mental Health
- Medicard / free GP visits: three free doctor or nurse visits per year for every New Zealander at enrolled practices, tracked via card and app. Excludes services already free (immunisations), after-hours and ACC visits. Cost $393.3m in 2027/28 rising to about $553m/yr. Launches July 2028. https://www.labour.org.nz/medicard
- Independent Pricing Authority for general practice funding by July 2028, citing a 7.6% GP funding shortfall. https://www.labour.org.nz/medicard
- Free prescriptions (removes $5 co-payment for all currently publicly eligible people) from 1 July 2027; cost $74.5m/yr; claims 155,000 people did not collect prescribed medicine for cost reasons. https://www.labour.org.nz/prescriptions
- Free cervical screening ages 25-69 from 1 Oct 2027; $21.6m first full year from existing baselines. https://www.labour.org.nz/cervicalscreening
- Free maternity scans nationwide from July 2028 (details not retrieved). https://www.labour.org.nz/policy
- Family Doctor Loan Scheme: loans up to 90% of practice purchase cost, max $500,000 per doctor, 10 years, interest-free for 2 years then 3%; up to 50 loans/yr, owner-operated practices only, from 1 July 2027; fiscal cost under $8m/yr. https://www.labour.org.nz/familydoctorloanscheme
- Graduate Nurse Job Guarantee: job offers for about 2,400 nursing graduates a year, min 0.8 FTE, from 2027; $525m from Vote Health cost-pressure allocation. https://www.labour.org.nz/graduate-nurse-job-guarantee
- Hospital repair backlog: $200m capital for Health NZ remediation (18 of 152 projects done at 31 Mar 2026 per Labour). https://www.labour.org.nz/start-work-now
- Fiscal plan includes $15.5b additional health funding over the forecast period. https://www.labour.org.nz/fiscal-plan
- Pharmac, mental health, Te Aka Whai Ora: no Labour policy found (UNVERIFIED / not announced as of this research).
- Independent assessment: none retrieved for Medicard. National argues a $1.5b health cost-pressure gap in 2030/31 is unfunded (partisan). https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/1733687/labour-s-fiscal-plan-national-s-claims-and-how-labour-is-defending-it

## 2. Education
- Start Work Now: about 2,000 schools get building/grounds work; $400m capital for state schools plus $50m operating for integrated schools. https://www.labour.org.nz/start-work-now
- Cutting Student Debt: 10% cut to eligible NZ-based loan balances (not for Fees Free beneficiaries) on 1 Apr 2027 for those finished study; others after 3 years in NZ post-graduation; balances of $2,000 or less wiped; about $40m/yr fair-value cost ($420m in fiscal plan). https://www.labour.org.nz/cutting-student-debt ; https://www.labour.org.nz/fiscal-plan
- Reported (UNVERIFIED, Wikipedia only): pause on NCEA abolition and curriculum changes; restore school lunch programme and expand to 400 additional schools; reinstate school boards' Treaty obligations. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Zealand_general_election
- Teacher pay, early childhood, charter schools: not found (UNVERIFIED). Pay equity restoration would affect teachers (see section 6).
- Independent assessment: none retrieved.

## 5. Cost of Living and Economy
- Capital Gains Tax: 28% on profit from sale of commercial and residential investment property; applies only to gains after 1 July 2027 (valuation at higher of cost or 1 July 2027 value); capital improvements deductible; exempt: family home, lifestyle blocks, farms, inheritances, KiwiSaver, shares, businesses; small businesses relocating to larger premises exempt. All revenue ring-fenced to health (Medicard, prescriptions). Labour says 9 in 10 New Zealanders will not pay. Revenue estimate not retrieved. https://www.labour.org.nz/capitalgainstax
- Fiscal plan (4 Oct 2026): OBEGAL surplus in 2028/29 (standard OBEGAL rather than OBEGALx); net debt below 20% of GDP over time; operating allowance held at $2.4b per Budget; $12b capital allowances over three budgets, about $4.1-4.2b used, about $8b unallocated; $2.9b Kainga Ora; $420m student loan write-off; $200m Future Fund investment; three-year fuel tax freeze; Hall Chadwick reviewed commitments as "reasonable assessments, conditional on the underpinning assumptions". https://www.labour.org.nz/fiscal-plan ; https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/1718141/labour-releases-its-fiscal-plan-promises-pay-rise-for-care-and-support-workers
- NZ Future Fund: separate from NZ Super Fund, governed by Guardians; seeded from Crown assets plus capital contribution; invests in infrastructure and innovative businesses; seeded assets protected in law; no size stated. https://www.labour.org.nz/futurefund
- Grocery: split Foodstuffs and Woolworths wholesale arms into independently operated wholesalers, end restrictive contracts, ban junk fees, guarantee wholesale access to smaller grocers. Price-gouging ban for large firms with market power. https://www.labour.org.nz/fairer-grocery-prices ; https://www.labour.org.nz/policy
- Fair Go for Drivers/Families: fuel price reporting, right to repair for post-2021 cars, ban secret finance commissions at car yards, 14-day penalty-free car purchase cancellation, 3-year fuel tax freeze; ban hidden fees and junk insurance. https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/1739653/labour-promises-right-to-repair-for-cars
- KiwiSaver: compulsory employer contributions from 1 July 2028 rising to 6% by 2032; default employee rate 4%; ban total-remuneration contracts. https://www.labour.org.nz/strengthening-kiwisaver
- Wealth tax, income tax, GST, Reserve Bank mandate, PBO: no policy found (UNVERIFIED).
- National's critique (Willis): pay equity $11b Treasury estimate leaves Labour "$8.5b short"; fuel tax freeze $3.1b; fare cap $260m; Future Fund lost dividends $2.8b; $1.5b health gap 2030/31; graduate nurses $526m. Labour reply (Edmonds/Hipkins): pay equity costs withheld to avoid undermining negotiations; fuel/transport items come from the National Land Transport Fund; health spend reduces ED costs; nurses covered by health cost-pressure funding. "Nine new taxes"/"hidden bill" framing not located in fetched pages (UNVERIFIED). https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/1733687/labour-s-fiscal-plan-national-s-claims-and-how-labour-is-defending-it
- Independent assessment: economist Cameron Bagrie said "it doesn't matter which political party you look at, there's going to be gaps"; both parties underfund roads and avoid hard tax choices (same RNZ link). Hall Chadwick review is commissioned by Labour. No Treasury/PBO costing of the CGT retrieved.

## 6. Employment
- Pay equity: restore legislation within first 100 days; $4/hour immediate rise for about 65,000 care and support workers from 1 Jan 2027 ($2.5b over four years); later settlements negotiated and funded in future budgets. https://www.labour.org.nz/pay-equity ; https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political/1718141/labour-releases-its-fiscal-plan-promises-pay-rise-for-care-and-support-workers
- Start Work Now: nearly 30,000 apprentices get $1,000 toolbox grant; construction and infrastructure jobs. https://www.labour.org.nz/start-work-now
- Apprenticeship Boost expansion: avg $56.5m/yr over four years; employer $500/month support extended from one to two years from 1 July 2028; five new trades from 1 July 2027. https://www.labour.org.nz/apprenticeshipboost
- Graduate nurse guarantee (section 1). Small Business Action Plan (payment times, paperwork). https://www.labour.org.nz/policy
- Minimum wage, Fair Pay Agreements, benefit settings: not found (UNVERIFIED).
- Independent assessment: Treasury estimate of $11b for full pay equity restoration is cited by National, not independently verified here.

## 7. Transport and Infrastructure
- $20/week public transport cap in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch; $10/week elsewhere; buses, trains, select ferries; from 1 July 2027; funded by a contribution from the National Land Transport Fund; average saving about $25/week. https://www.labour.org.nz/farecap . Cost estimates of $91-112m/yr (Wikipedia, UNVERIFIED); National says $260m.
- Fuel tax increases frozen three years (above RNZ link). Unmanned petrol stations enabled; NZTA land to independent fuel retailers.
- Rail, roads, EVs, light rail: no Labour policy found (UNVERIFIED). Hipkins called National's roading approach "farcical".
- Independent assessment: none retrieved.

## 8. Te Tiriti o Waitangi and Māori
- Backing Māori into Skilled Work: $21m/yr contestable grants to Māori providers (marae, iwi trusts); builds on prior programme (about 3,000 participants, 72% employed by Sept 2024). https://www.labour.org.nz/maori-trades-training
- Treaty Principles Bill, Treaty clauses review, co-governance, Te Aka Whai Ora, Māori wards, Whānau Ora: no 2026 policy page found (UNVERIFIED). Wikipedia claims reinstating school boards' Treaty obligations (UNVERIFIED).
- Independent assessment: none retrieved.

## Update 6 Oct 2026 (pages supplied by the project owner; Wikipedia election page)
- Fiscal plan page confirms: OBEGAL (not OBEGALx) surplus 2028/29; net debt toward 20% of GDP over time; $2.4b operating allowance; $12b capital allowances with $4.1b used; $15.5b additional health over the forecast period; fare cap funded by reprioritising 1% of the National Land Transport Fund; Hall Chadwick review commissioned by Labour. RNZ (5 Oct): Labour says it holds $10.5b of unallocated funding against National's $8.5b pay-equity gap claim.
- CGT page confirms 28%, investment and commercial property only, gains after 1 July 2027, exemptions as listed; no revenue estimate is published on the page.
- Medicard page confirms cost schedule ($393.3m in 2027/28 rising to $553m) and the Independent Pricing Authority (GP underfunding 7.6%, ~$137m, per 2022 analysis).
- 1News (21 Sep 2026): Labour ruled out changing interest deductibility for landlords.
- Wikipedia election page: Labour will repeal the Regulatory Standards Act within 100 days; reinstate school boards' Treaty obligations and reverse the coalition's curriculum changes and mandated testing (April 2026); Labour and the Greens announced at Waitangi (Feb 2026) that they would work together in the campaign and in government; Labour contests all seven Māori electorates.
- PREFU 2026 (Treasury, 29 Sep): OBEGALx -$6.8b 2026/27, -$0.8b 2027/28, +$4.0b 2028/29; OBEGAL +$2.1b in 2028/29; net core Crown debt peaks 43.9% of GDP in 2027/28; CPI 4.1% June 2026 falling to 1.9%; unemployment peak 5.6%.
- Pass-2 crawl: Hipkins ruled out interest-deductibility changes on 21 Sep 2026 to avoid giving landlords "the excuse of increasing rents"; Treasury's 2024 Regulatory Impact Statement found rents are driven by incomes and supply rather than tax settings (b2bnews.co.nz, 21-22 Sep 2026). Fiscal plan vs National (The Spinoff, 30 Sep): surplus 2028/29 $2.0b vs $2.1b; core Crown expenses 30.4% vs 29.8% of GDP in 2030/31; net debt $876m higher; only one pay-equity settlement ($2.5b) costed, the rest from $10.5b unallocated headroom; Treasury put the 2025 pay-equity savings at $12.8b over four years. Labour formally ruled out NZ First on 10 Aug 2026.
- Pass-2 crawl: Labour will scrap youth military-style academies as a 'failed experiment' (Stuff; Oranga Tamariki data: 80% of pilot participants reoffended within a year, the youth-justice average); justice spokesperson Camilla Belich calls the coalition's crime laws 'bumper stickers' but won't commit to repealing Three Strikes or the gang laws (NZ Herald). Labour will not re-establish Te Aka Whai Ora; it will fund Maori providers directly through Iwi Maori Partnership Boards (Te Ao Maori News, 1 Sep 2026). Hipkins has ruled out taxes other than the narrow CGT; National's 'nine new taxes' line bundles partner parties' policies (RNZ). CGT expected to raise ~$700m a year over the forecast period (1News).
