# New Zealand First - 2026 Policy and Track Record Research

Compiled 2026-10-06 for the 7 November 2026 general election.

**Method limits (read first):** The WebSearch budget was exhausted before research began, so this was compiled from WebFetch only (nzfirst.nz policy pages, Wikipedia, RNZ/NZ Herald index pages). Many reputable-outlet articles and independent analyses (Treasury, NZIER, Infometrics, Commerce Commission, etc.) could NOT be retrieved. Where no independent source was fetched, this is marked UNVERIFIED. nzfirst.nz page contents are party statements, not verified facts. Wikipedia pages fetched contained some internal inconsistencies (e.g. a donation figure dated "October 24, 2026", a future date), so Wikipedia-derived items are treated as lower confidence.

## Party snapshot
- Leader: Winston Peters (founder). Deputy leader: Shane Jones (elected September 2025, per Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_First
- 2023 result: 6.08% party vote, 8 seats; part of National-ACT-NZ First coalition since 2023. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_First
- Polling: 9% in the 1News-Verian poll of 23-27 Sept 2026; 2026 range about 7-16% (14-15% in July). Several polls show a hung-parliament scenario with NZ First as potential kingmaker (left bloc Labour-Greens-TPM 61-65 seats vs National-ACT-NZ First 61-67). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_for_the_2026_New_Zealand_general_election
- Coalition stance/rule-outs: Wikipedia says the party ruled out Labour "as stated before the 2023 election" and Peters says NZ First stays open to negotiations; no explicit 2026 rule-out statements (Labour or Te Pāti Māori) were found. UNVERIFIED for 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_First
- Immigration portfolio: party policy says an immigration ministerial portfolio "must go to a New Zealand First minister" in coalition. https://www.nzfirst.nz/immigration_reform_policies
- 4 Oct 2026 (RNZ campaign roundup): Peters accused coalition partners of "copy-nating" NZ First policy. https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/political
- Peters has invoked the coalition "agree to disagree" clause multiple times (COVID inquiry leadership, trade agreements), per Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_First

### Sept-Oct 2026 campaign announcements (dated per https://www.nzfirst.nz/news)
- 6 Sep: NZ Super for citizens only from 2029
- 11 Sep: Immigration reform package
- 20 Sep: "Make NZ Healthy" (first of a larger health plan)
- 24 Sep: 20% company tax rate for SMEs
- 26 Sep: Abolish Waitangi Tribunal ("make-work industry")
- 30 Sep: Peters criticises Chloe Swarbrick over Paris Accord comments
- Also from August: "Use It or Lose It" dairy policy; supermarket duopoly breakup; gentailer split; Wikipedia also lists KiwiSaver contributions to 10%, a migrant values statement, and a ban on private fireworks sales (UNVERIFIED on official site).

## 1. Health and Mental Health
- "Make NZ Healthy": shift focus from treating illness to prevention, with metabolic health as the driver of chronic disease. 15 policy points including nutrition education in primary and medical schools, independent review of dietary guidelines, cutting GP administrative burden, health coaches in primary care, funding NZ research on nutrition and mental illness, assessing IV vitamin C in surgery, reviewing vitamin D levels, backing agricultural innovation/organics. No budget figures or timelines; described as "just the first campaign announcement". https://www.nzfirst.nz/the_plan_to_make_nz_healthy
- Hospital funding, Pharmac, Gold Card: no NZ First-specific 2026 policy found. UNVERIFIED.
- Mental health: only the nutrition-research item above found. UNVERIFIED beyond that.
- KiwiSaver Generation (see section 5) and Super restriction (section 6) relate to seniors.
- **Independent assessment:** none retrieved. UNVERIFIED. (The dietary-guideline and IV vitamin C items are contested clinically; no source fetched.)

## 3. Crime and Justice
- Track record: coalition adopted NZ First's policy of training 500 new police officers; further 470 new corrections officers and district gang units announced (Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_National_Government_of_New_Zealand
- Immigration: deportation expanded to any imprisonable offence, automatic deportation for violent, sexual, fraud, organised crime and migrant-exploitation offences. https://www.nzfirst.nz/immigration_reform_policies
- 2026 sentencing, gangs, youth justice, rehabilitation policy: not found. UNVERIFIED.
- **Independent assessment:** none retrieved (Ministry of Justice/Police delivery data not checked). UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Housing and Rental Costs
- Immigration link: social housing to prioritise citizens. https://www.nzfirst.nz/immigration_reform_policies
- Marsden Point Special Economic Zone: RMA consents and planning rules decided by a government panel; considered tax breaks and amended Overseas Investment Act provisions. https://www.nzfirst.nz/establishing_a_special_economic_zone_at_marsden_point
- Track record: Fast-track Approvals Act 2024 (Royal Assent 23 Dec 2024); by Oct 2024, 149 projects listed for the process including 44 housing developments (Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast-track_Approvals_Act_2024
- Foreign buyers, first-home buyers, rent policy: not found. UNVERIFIED.
- **Independent assessment:** Controller and Auditor-General and Chief Ombudsman raised concerns about the Fast-track Act's "enormous executive powers" and limited transparency (per Wikipedia summary; primary source not fetched). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast-track_Approvals_Act_2024. No housing-supply effect assessment found. UNVERIFIED.

## 5. Cost of Living and Economy
- **SME tax:** company tax rate cut from 28% to 20% for firms with turnover under $30m; party estimates initial cost about $1bn a year, argues growth offsets it; cites OECD research and Australia. https://www.nzfirst.nz/20_tax_rate_for_smes
- **$100bn NZ Future Fund:** ring-fenced from politicians, multi-decade domestic infrastructure only, tax incentives to attract investors; modelled on Singapore, Ireland, Nordics; adopted at 2024 convention; no timeline. https://www.nzfirst.nz/the_100bn_new_zealand_future_fund
- **Supermarkets:** split Foodstuffs into two competing co-ops (New World/Four Square; Pak'nSave); Commerce Commission penalties raised to Australian levels (greater of $10m, 3x gain, or 10% of turnover); Groceries Commissioner with investigation/penalty powers; new Commerce Act framework on "farm to shelf". Cites Commerce Commission finding of about $1m a day in excess profit (party claim). https://www.nzfirst.nz/break_up_the_supermarket_duopoly
- **Gentailers:** separate generation from retail; stop the most expensive generator setting price for all; long-term fixed-price contracts for new generation; solar owners paid same price they pay. Claims big four control almost 90% of generation. https://www.nzfirst.nz/breaking_up_the_gentailers
- **NZ-owned bank:** buy BNZ from NAB, merge with Kiwibank as "National Bank of New Zealand"; funded by retail/KiwiSaver bonds, Crown debt, limited Future Fund/ACC money; party says BNZ earns over $1.5bn a year ("self-financing in expectation"). https://www.nzfirst.nz/backing_a_new_zealand_owned_bank
- **KiwiSaver Generation:** compulsory enrolment at birth for citizens with $1,000 Crown contribution; contribution rates to 8% rising to 10%. https://www.nzfirst.nz/establishing_a_kiwisaver_generation
- **Kiwi Kids Grant:** $5,000 a year tax-free for three years per child, first three children, at least one citizen parent; about $400m a year. https://www.nzfirst.nz/the_kiwi_kids_grant
- **Dairy:** raise independent-processor milk access cap from 50m to 500m litres a year (Dairy Industry Restructuring Act review, minister reporting 2027). https://www.nzfirst.nz/use_it_or_lose_it_dairy_policy
- **Fuel security:** $1bn National Subsurface Development Survey; royalties above 50% at production maturity into a sovereign wealth fund; claims a commercial discovery odds "well above ninety percent" (party claim); production possible within 6 years. https://www.nzfirst.nz/future_proofing_our_fuel_security
- **Immigration settings:** see Employment (section 6).
- GST off food, Reserve Bank positions: not found. UNVERIFIED.
- **Independent assessment:** none retrieved for any of these (Treasury, NZIER, Infometrics, Commerce Commission, Electricity Authority). UNVERIFIED. The fiscal costings above are party estimates only.

## 6. Employment
- **Immigration reform (11 Sep 2026):** citizenship qualifying period 5 to 10 years minimum; permanent residence 2 to 5 years; 10-year welfare stand-down for all visa holders; 24-month overstayer amnesty to self-deport; resident visa cap of 20,000 a year; 30-year population plan; birthright citizenship only for children of citizens; student visa holders lose automatic work rights; higher application fees. https://www.nzfirst.nz/immigration_reform_policies
- **NZ Super citizens only from 2029** (three-year implementation window); party says super for older migrants costs about $1bn a year, roughly 42,000 recipients over 50 (Dec 2025). https://www.nzfirst.nz/nz_super_for_citizens_only
- **Regional jobs:** 50% of mining royalties returned to regions, reopen School of Mines, longer permits. https://www.nzfirst.nz/returning_50_of_mining_royalties_to_the_regions
- Citizens-only voting (permanent residents currently vote after 2 years). https://www.nzfirst.nz/citizens_only_voting
- Minimum wage, worker protections: not found. UNVERIFIED.
- **Independent assessment:** none retrieved. UNVERIFIED.

## 7. Transport and Infrastructure
- **Regional Infrastructure Fund:** $1.2bn fund from NZ First's coalition policy (Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_National_Government_of_New_Zealand (Wikipedia page for the fund itself returned 404.)
- **Interislander:** Finance Minister Willis declined KiwiRail's request for an extra $1.47bn for ferry replacement, seeking cheaper alternatives. NZ First's own position not retrieved. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_National_Government_of_New_Zealand
- **Marsden Point SEZ**, with Drydock, Northland Expressway and rail links. https://www.nzfirst.nz/establishing_a_special_economic_zone_at_marsden_point
- **Energy/mining/oil and gas:** items in section 5; Fast-track Act (Jones: "blind frog, goodbye, Freddy"); 11 mining projects among the 149 listed. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast-track_Approvals_Act_2024
- **Paris Accord withdrawal:** claims up to $22bn a year goes offshore (party claim; UNVERIFIED), NZ is 0.17% of emissions; pledged as a central election issue. https://www.nzfirst.nz/withdraw_from_paris_accord
- Christchurch Cathedral: $15m additional funding (per site policy index). https://www.nzfirst.nz/policy
- EV, public transport positions: not found. UNVERIFIED.
- **Independent assessment:** Auditor-General/Ombudsman concerns on Fast-track (above). Forest & Bird warned of possible breach of UK/EU FTA obligations (Wikipedia). No RIF evaluation or Paris-withdrawal cost assessment retrieved. UNVERIFIED.

## 8. Te Tiriti o Waitangi and Māori Issues
- **Abolish Waitangi Tribunal:** legislation within first 100 days of next term; replaced by a Treaty of Waitangi Completion Commission to finalise claims filed before 2008, then close; Crown-Māori work returns to Ministry of Māori Development. https://www.nzfirst.nz/ending_the_waitangi_makework_industry
- **Referendum on Māori seats** after the next election; cites 27% of Parliament of Māori descent and about 50% of eligible Māori on the Māori roll. https://www.nzfirst.nz/referendum_on_maori_seats
- **Disestablish Auckland Council's Independent Māori Statutory Board** via member's bill; cites $3.5m ratepayer cost. https://www.nzfirst.nz/disestablish_auckland_s_independent_maori_statutory_board
- **Treaty Principles Bill:** National and NZ First did not support it beyond first reading (Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_National_Government_of_New_Zealand
- **Māori wards:** coalition legislated local referendums on establishing/retaining Māori wards (Wikipedia). Same URL.
- Citizens-only voting and English as an official language ("English Language Act 2026" per Wikipedia; UNVERIFIED) fit the same agenda. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_First
- Māori Health Authority, Whānau Ora, Treaty clauses review: not retrieved. UNVERIFIED.
- Rule-out of Te Pāti Māori: not found. UNVERIFIED.
- **Independent assessment:** none retrieved (Waitangi Tribunal/Crown Law/Ministry of Justice). UNVERIFIED.
